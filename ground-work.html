12-Week Roadmap

Mon / Wed / Fri — Core & Plyo circuit (rotates through four templates so it never repeats back-to-back). Tuesday — pre-practice ball mastery, rotating through three skill focuses. Thursday — a short, max-effort explosive speed session (acceleration and elastic power) plus a rotating agility or flexibility block; this is deliberately not a conditioning session, since MLS Next practices and games already supply that. Saturday flexes for game day or active recovery; Sunday is full rest. After Week 12, the program holds at Phase 3 intensity as your maintenance level.

Built for solo, no-weight training — bodyweight, a loaded backpack, stairs, a mat, a resistance band and a soccer ball. Progress is saved automatically on this device.
